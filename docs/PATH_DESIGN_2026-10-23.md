## How the round ran

Seven read-only mappers walked the paths as built (dine-in, to-go, grocery, counter, kitchen and expo, arrival/account/after, manager and the staff shell) and one extracted the visual system; a lead designer picked eight moments the first round had not designed, excluding m1–m12 and the sixty decided IDs unless the as-built code showed friction the record had not foreseen. Three concept agents per moment (Quiet · Guided · Glanceable, two screens each) and a three-seat judge panel per moment followed; the 48 concept artboards were drawn from their briefs. The owner was asked once. One agent per moment then synthesized a refined spec from the judges' pick, the grafts and the recorded defaults, checking every product claim against `09c1c2a`; ONE consistency agent read all eight specs and set the shared vocabulary below; an adversarial critic reviewed each amended spec; the refined screens were drawn with the critic's blocking fixes applied. Each spec ends in appendices (A) the consistency amendments, (B) the critic's blocking fixes, (C) its suggestions — **where an appendix contradicts the spec body, the appendix wins**, and where two specs' appendices disagree, *Cross-spec reconciliations* below wins over both. The infrastructure facts of the run (four CPU cores cap a workflow at two concurrent agents; two container restarts; one usage-limit pause) are in the PR, not here.

**Burmese:** every new Burmese string in the specs is a draft for the native sitting (K15), never final copy. Reused shipped strings are cited to their file and line. Strings with no draft stay English-only and are listed in each spec.

## The judges' scores

### 13 · The to-go checkout's Who-and-When slip — asked once, validated before Pay, bilingual, with Pay in reach — guided, elevated

**Spec:** [`path-design-2026-10-23/m13-togo-slip.md`](path-design-2026-10-23/m13-togo-slip.md) · **OPEN-ITEMS:** PD14 · **Builds it:** diner-cart · money-rails (the one `PICKUP_CONTACT_MESSAGES` constant in create-intent) · **Device:** phone

**Who:** A to-go guest on their own phone — first-timer or regular; a Burmese-first guest ordering for parents. Owned by Phase 3e (J24, PHASE3B_DESIGN.md:69 'the Who/When slip'): filed, not built, UX unsettled.
