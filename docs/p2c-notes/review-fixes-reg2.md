  re-reads — any later read settles it, and a different figure is said with both amounts; a lost

## DESIGN-LANGUAGE rule changes

Against the drafts integration merges from `docs/p2c-notes/register.md` §1 (§29, §17) and
`docs/p2c-notes/gate.md` §1 (§17) — `docs/DESIGN-LANGUAGE.md` itself does not carry them yet.

- **§29 (the quote)** — replace "The confirm then quotes the server's figure until the page catches up
  (the quote's `basis`)" with: "…until the page catches up — its live figure reaching the server's, OR
  any committed read that STARTED after the refusal (the quote carries the page's read clock,
  `raisedAt`); a read already in the air at the refusal settles nothing. A live figure that then
  differs from the quote is a drift, said with both figures."
- **§29 (a lost response)** — add: "The counter's 'most likely went through' hold ENDS: a read that
  started after the settle could last land (`SETTLE_MAY_LAND_MS`, the freeze's lifetime) and shows the
  order open, or a reader collect that starts, proves the cash never landed."
- **§17 (the one region)** — add to the precedence note: "Every line is a setter and clears the ones
  above it that describe an older moment — including the reader's status, whose every change clears a
  standing writeError (a refusal from before the collect never masks a money sentence)."
- **§17 (focus)** — add: "A programmatic focus target (a heading the page lands on) never carries an
  inline `outline: none` — the global `:focus-visible` ring is how a keyboard user sees the landing."
- **§17 (refused taps)** — add: "A refused tap re-says its reason on EVERY tap: the region's text is
  keyed on a per-refusal number, so a repeated sentence is a new node (a same-value `setState` is a
  React no-op and says nothing)."

## Owner-visible behaviour changes

  finished in 10 minutes, ask the owner to check the card payments before you take payment again."
